    03 // ABOUT & CONTACT

    I'm Jose, the person behind b0nyo. I got into security because I always wanted to know how things worked and why they broke. I like the part of the job where there is no obvious answer and I have to keep digging until something makes sense.

    Most of my time goes into web and network exploitation, privilege escalation, post-exploitation, and Active Directory. I started this blog to keep a record of what I learn and share it with anyone who may find it useful. You can contact me if you want to talk about security, research, or a project.
